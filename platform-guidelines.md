# Platform Guidelines

To make a platform viable for inclusion into Pipeline, it has to adhere to certain content guidelines.
In particular, we will reject inclusion of platforms that contain a significant amount of the following content:

- Illegal content, in either the EU or US law.
- Copyrighted content without consent of the copyright holder/publisher.
- Pornographic content.
- Misinformation, conspiracy theories.
- Discrimination.
- Cryptocurrency content.

This list may be amended in the future, as more platforms with content we do not desire are discovered.

While likely all platforms contain some content of the above type, there is a large difference between such content being moderated, and the content being able to flourish on the platform.
As a rule-of-thumb, if the platform is well-known for any of the above content, it will likely be rejected.

## Rejected Platforms

The following is a list of platforms that have been considered and were rejected.
Note however that platforms and their content can change.
If there is a significant reduction of the prohibited content listed above (which seems unlikely though), the platforms may be reconsidered.

- LBRY/Odysee
- Rumble
- BitChute

## Why

You may ask why Pipeline has such rules in the first place, for example by thinking "when the user does not want content of a platform, the user should just turn it off".
There are a few reasons for that:

- As the developers of Pipeline, we do not want to help promote and spread above content.
- Most users likely do not want to see such content.
  Having it show up in search results is therefore not desired.
- Regarding illegal content, this would likely lead to legal problems for Pipeline.
- Regarding pornographic content, this will require having an age-rating of Pipeline.

While "having the platforms turned off by default" or "have some plugin system" may alleviate some of the concerns, we are still not comfortable with Pipeline serving any of the above content.

## Not Happy with the Guidelines

Feel free to discuss these guidelines with us, as long as you can remain respectful and adhere to the [GNOME Code of Conduct](https://conduct.gnome.org/).
The developers of Pipeline always have the last word about whether a platform will be included or not.
If you disagree with our choice, feel free to fork Pipeline (while keeping in mind the [license](/LICENSE)).
